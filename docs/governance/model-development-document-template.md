# ATLAS Model Development Document Template

## Modes

ATLAS supports three model-document modes:

| Mode | Audience | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Executive Summary | Senior leadership and business reviewers | Business objective, target, champion model, key metrics, risks, readiness checklist, and material decisions |
| Full Governance Package | Model Risk Management, validation, and compliance | Executive content plus preprocessing, candidate models, interpretation, monitoring, decision journal appendix, and experiment appendix |
| Technical Appendix | Data science and implementation teams | Full governance package plus reproducibility, notebook, and technical artifacts |

## Required Sections

1. Cover Page
2. Executive Summary
3. Report Readiness Checklist
4. Material Decision Summary
5. Business Context
6. Data Description
7. Data Handling and Preprocessing
8. Candidate Models
9. Champion Performance
10. Interpretation
11. Model Selection Rationale
12. Limitations and Risks
13. Monitoring Recommendations
14. Appendices as applicable

## Readiness Expectations

ATLAS should clearly flag missing target, missing champion, absent model metrics, absent decision rationale, and stale or cross-problem experiment records. Raw click history belongs in the Decision Journal, not the executive summary.
