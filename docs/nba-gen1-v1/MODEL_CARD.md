## Small model screen and locked selection rule

Six configurations across four families are implemented in [models.py](C:/Users/james/asp-model-studio/server/sports_nba_gen1/models.py).

| Family | Configuration |
|---|---|
| League/home-court benchmark | Earlier-game mean home margin and mean total |
| Chronological Elo benchmark | Earlier-game linear association of margin with home-minus-away Elo; earlier mean total |
| Regularized linear | Joint margin/total Ridge, training-median imputation and scaling; alpha 10 or 100 |
| Nonlinear | Separate margin and total histogram gradient boosting; L2 10 or 50; 150 iterations, learning rate 0.05, 15 leaves, minimum leaf size 40, no automatic early-stopping split |

All point outputs derive home and away coherently as `(total + margin)/2` and `(total − margin)/2`. The fitted preprocessor and model are retained together. League/Elo/Ridge artifacts support coefficient reporting with an associational interpretation. For the selected nonlinear setting, the pipeline uses one fixed permutation per feature on the last held-out Development point-prediction fold, measuring change in point mean-squared error. This is associational diagnostic importance, not energy-score importance, a causal effect or a selection input. No coefficients or importance values are reported now because no real model has been fitted.

Development chooses one setting per family. Validation starts with the league benchmark and considers Elo, Ridge and HGB in that fixed order. A candidate replaces the incumbent only if all of these predeclared checks pass on the same intact games:

- At least 1% lower joint energy score and a paired 95% interval whose upper endpoint is below zero.
- Log-loss deterioration no greater than 0.005 and binary-Brier deterioration no greater than 0.002.
- Each home, away, total and margin CRPS deterioration no greater than 1%.
- At 50%, 80% and 90%, absolute deviation from nominal coverage may worsen by at most 0.02; interval-width ratios must remain between 0.9 and 1.2.
- Energy must not worsen in either chronological half of Validation.

Calibration and blending are disabled for this first screen. Winner accuracy or hypothetical betting profit cannot select a model. No configuration has passed or failed these gates on real NBA games yet.

## Joint distribution and tie treatment

The planned population is **10,000 original paired HOME/AWAY draws per configuration and game**. Donors are complete oriented residual pairs from chronological out-of-fold point predictions, with at least 200 eligible earlier donors. No in-sample residual bank or independent team-error sampling is accepted. Residuals are centered by their pooled home and away means once.

Simulation floors negative latent scores at zero, records that activation, and uses stochastic integer rounding. An integer tie receives a complete sampled pair of points from eligible observed overtime periods, repeating only while tied. At least 20 authenticated earlier overtime periods are required by the runner, including resolving evidence. If the safeguard is exhausted, the entire forecast is unsupported; tied draws are not discarded or assigned artificial winners.

The residual donors already describe full games. Appending an empirical overtime period is an explicit tie-resolution rule for that final-score distribution; the pre-resolution scores are **not regulation-score predictions**. The rule's tie frequency, appended-period counts, score-mean changes and home-win probability changes are retained for assessment. This is a modeling limitation to evaluate, not a claimed correction.

Deterministic game seeds bind the game and configuration policy to the declared base seed, 20260913. Separate NumPy PCG64 streams handle residual-pair sampling, rounding and overtime. The original joint draws, distributions, source bindings, counts and simulation diagnostics are retained. Monte Carlo standard errors quantify sampling precision; they do not bound model, source or feature error.
