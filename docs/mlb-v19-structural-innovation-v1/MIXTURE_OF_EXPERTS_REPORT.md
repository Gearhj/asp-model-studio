# Mixture-of-experts report

No mixture was fit. A valid MoE requires at least one specialist that passes its own locked proper-score gate and enough chronological routing evidence to estimate gating without outcome-driven regime search. This program produced one assessed candidate and it failed its gate. Existing archetype candidates remain chronology-blocked; bullpen and manager specialists remain data-blocked.

Fitting a gate here would add degrees of freedom around a null result and would violate the bounded candidate program. The permitted future design is hierarchical shrinkage toward V18 with explicit missing-evidence fallback and a validation-learned gate, after the baseline chronology repair and after an individual specialist demonstrates incremental value.
