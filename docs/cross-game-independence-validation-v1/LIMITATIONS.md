# Limitations

- The study is reconstructed chronological research, not prospective certification. Physical per-game forecast creation and source-cutoff receipts are unavailable.
- NBA's retained Development rows lack exact tip times, so same-window analysis excludes NBA from exact timing categories.
- MLB is absent. No chronological whole-game Development forecast ledger was admitted, and sealed/Final OOT evidence was not opened. Doubleheaders and shared-team pairs therefore remain unsupported.
- Shared venue, geography, weather systems, uncertain players, roster links and source-failure incidents are not governed common fields across the ledgers.
- Postseason (297 pairs), sequential-incentive (36 pairs) and CFB–NFL (71 pairs) strata are too small for the frozen gate.
- Pair counts are combinatorial. Independent uncertainty comes from dates; highly uneven slate sizes reduce the pair-weighted effective date count.
- Raw joint calibration conflates dependence with each model's single-leg calibration. Small residual correlation does not validate a poorly calibrated product.
- Only two-leg same-date events were assessed. The evidence does not validate three-plus-leg multiplication.
- Totals and other market events were not assessed because these ledgers do not contain valid pre-outcome line-event probabilities.
- No causal conclusion about common weather, incentives or data faults follows from residual correlation.
- Previously inspected Validation evidence remains previously inspected; no result is a fresh champion, promotion or profitability claim.
