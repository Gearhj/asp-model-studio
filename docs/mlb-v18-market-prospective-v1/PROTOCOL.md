This is a new prospective protocol. It does not amend the September 18 retrospective wagering diagnosis, V18, the V2 acceptance protocol, consumer policy, source entitlements or production. The local frozen JSON and its SHA-256 are the authoritative instance; this document defines its rules. A later amendment requires a new identity and applies only to later enrollment. No earlier game can be added to repair coverage.

## Objective and authority

Estimate the paired predictive log-score difference between the exact raw V18 home-win forecast used at a pregame decision checkpoint and genuinely fresh, same-book two-sided moneyline probabilities. Describe selected and nonselected games separately while making the full eligible population primary. Model/market disagreement and realized losses alone establish neither an implementation defect nor a wagering opportunity.

Use only already authorized retained producer and consumer captures. This protocol authorizes no provider request, paid data, new acquisition owner, publication into production discovery, consumer import, calibration, policy or model change. Enrollment and assessment remain private research. There is no new scheduler in this delivery. The consumer-side evidence export must be acknowledged and produce genuine pregame packets before enrollment can begin. A frozen protocol is not evidence of operating collection.

## Fresh market construction

Primary market: full-game two-way home/away moneyline including extra innings, with matching settlement and pitcher/action conventions. Both legs must share game, canonical provider, canonical book, market, period, conventions and a retained provider event/market snapshot (response ID/checksum). Require an authoritative bookmaker/market observation/update clock and a locally established availability bound for each leg, each no later than the anchor. Fetch/ingestion time cannot replace a missing quote-update time. Preserve per-leg update timestamps and skew: same response does not mean the two legs updated simultaneously. In particular, the current SportsGameOdds `available_as_of=fetch.requested_at` is not proof of response receipt or database commit; use a completed raw-response/consumer receipt bound by the anchor.

Each leg's age at the anchor must be between **0 and 15 minutes inclusive**. Negative ages, missing clocks, one-sided quotes, suspended/closed markets, invalid prices, conflicting orientation, unproved conventions, different source snapshots or absent availability evidence are not evaluable. Even if policy permits 240 minutes, a stale pair remains unpriced for this study. An unchanged quote with an old update clock remains old; repoll time does not refresh it.

For each eligible book/provider pair, choose its latest complete eligible source snapshot by older-leg observation time, then by local availability time, then stable snapshot ID ascending. Choose the **lexicographically first canonical book key, then provider key** among eligible pairs. Do not choose best odds, closest-to-model prices or the book selected by the policy. Retain all other eligible books as sensitivity/dispersion metadata, not independent games or an alternative primary selected after results. The primary rule is identical for selected and nonselected games. Preserve a separately labeled locked-position-book diagnostic if it differs.

Convert American prices to decimal exactly; require decimal >1. Set implied probabilities `rH=1/dH`, `rA=1/dA`, then `qH=rH/(rH+rA)` and `qA=1-qH`. Preserve original prices, pair overround and book keys. No exchange/prediction-market prices, consensus interpolation, closing-price substitution, mixed-book pairs, stale carry-forward or imputed price enters the primary metric.

If either leg is missing or fails a rule, label `UNPRICED` with the exact reason; keep the legitimate V18 forecast and selection state for coverage. Do not treat missing prices as zero edge or a model failure. Report price coverage by date, selection state and book, including the count of games lacking any eligible book.

Optional forecast-time and closing diagnostics are distinct phases with the existing maximum ages of 30 and 15 minutes. They require their own genuine retained clocks and cannot replace the decision-time primary pair. Closing uses the last eligible pair strictly before actual start, same primary book/provider, and is descriptive only. No additional acquisition is requested to fill it.

## Outcomes, pushes and metric

Read no outcomes during admission. After genuine finals, bind V18 and market to the **same admitted consumer outcome revision**, its source identity, admission/final clocks and immutable hash. Never infer completion from scheduled start. Corrections produce an append-only assessment revision retaining the original; forecasts and selection labels do not change.

Primary endpoint per decisive completed game is `logLoss(V18 pHome,yHome) - logLoss(market qHome,yHome)`; negative favors V18. Use natural logs and equal weight per game. For numerical scoring only, probabilities are bounded to [1e-15,1-1e-15], with every affected count/value disclosed; saved probabilities remain unchanged.

The primary moneyline comparison is conditional on an officially decisive result. A true tie, push, refund, no-contest or void is explicitly retained but excluded from the binary endpoint for **both** forecasts. Count each separately; never score a push as a loss or infer a result from a position's profit. Markets with incompatible settlement conventions are excluded before enrollment. Suspended/unresolved games remain pending until an admitted final, or unresolved at closeout.

Secondary diagnostics: paired Brier difference, unadjusted model and market log loss/Brier, fixed reliability bands [0,.4), [.4,.5), [.5,.6), [.6,1], win-probability disagreement, quote ages/lead times, cross-book dispersion, selection coverage, and original V18 score/margin/total errors and CRPS where the retained distribution and admitted final support them. A market moneyline has no score distribution; do not invent a market CRPS or joint energy score. No wagering-return, new Kelly, threshold, calibration fit or component search is part of this protocol.

Report selected versus evaluated-not-selected as predeclared exploratory strata; no subgroup superiority or selection-causality claim. Bands need 30 games; subgroups need 50 and at least 10 home wins/10 home losses to display comparative summaries as adequately supported. Below that, show counts and explicitly insufficient descriptive values. Unknown selection is separately counted, never merged into nonselected.
