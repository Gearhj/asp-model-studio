# Frozen-gate binding

The readiness contract binds the completed v1 study without changing it:

- design SHA-256: `5749b2457b52589a612b3a2597341185a5baf9b814d300715ec115a4c8dda87f`;
- historical result SHA-256: `fafc0b9bd9997e65fa6db209bceb6dacb91943528e136ed23603daf922ffef24`.

Coverage readiness requires at least 500 distinct games, 50 dates, 1,000 unique unordered eligible pairs and two reliability bands, each with 200 pairs and 20 dates. The other practical tolerances remain in the frozen study design and are applied only by a separately authorized assessment after readiness.

Readiness does not inspect outcomes, calculate correlations, score calibration or authorize AISP display. It only indicates that the predeclared coverage floor has been reached. No threshold may be changed after outcomes are observed.

Same-game pairs fail validation. Reverse order produces the same pair ID. Duplicates fail. Shared-participant pairs can be retained as excluded evidence but cannot count toward an unrelated-game category.
