# NFL M1 scoring and monitoring contract

Each completed game is one assessment unit. Simulation draws measure numerical precision and are not independent observations.

The controlling revision is the latest eligible forecast of the admitted M1 identity published strictly before kickoff. Import time cannot make a late forecast eligible. A corrected final creates a new, linked evaluation and preserves its predecessor.

Monitor:

- three-way log loss;
- three-way summed Brier score over HOME/AWAY/TIE (range 0 to 2);
- winner accuracy;
- home-score, away-score and pooled score error;
- margin and total error;
- reliability/calibration when sample size supports it;
- spread and total win/loss/push using stored compatible pregame lines;
- descriptive segments, without selecting routing rules from small samples.

The first operational review occurs at 50 settled governed games. The calibration review occurs at 100. The 50-game mark is a review gate, not a prerequisite for beginning production. Calibration graphics and claims remain suppressed until the declared support threshold is met.

AISP reported all 16 Week 2 games `READY_AFTER_GOVERNED_FINAL`, with final ingestion and scoring armed hourly. Settlement remains a separate system and does not define model quality.
