# Market information, contract and incentive feature specification V1

Contract: `MARKET_INFORMATION_CONTRACT_FEATURE_SPEC_V1`  
Companion: `docs/rff_component_contract_v1.json`  
Scope: editable research specification; MLB first, extensible to NFL, NBA and MMA. No forecast, policy, position or source-access authority is granted.

## Interpretation and point-in-time requirements

These are candidate information features, not established sources of predictive advantage. Prefer extraction of a specific, attributable news event over generic fan sentiment. Contracts primarily describe opportunity and usage, not presumed motivation or ability. Historical validation is possible only for records whose original publication time, first observed time, source scope and entity mapping can be established. A current webpage, corrected article or present-day depth chart is not an historical vintage.

Each observation must retain `itemId`, `sourceId`, `sourceRecordId`, `sourceUrlOrArchiveRef`, `sourcePublishedAtUtc`, `observedAtUtc`, `availableAsOfUtc`, `effectiveAtUtc` when known, `correctionOfItemId`, immutable content digest, sport/competition/season, canonical event/team/player identifiers, extracted assertion, reliability tier, extraction method/version and source-use restrictions. Publication is the original item's time, not the time an exporter ran. Retain updates as successors rather than overwriting the original assertion.

An input is usable at a checkpoint only if its publication, observation and availability times all precede or equal that checkpoint; the checkpoint must precede event start. An explicitly supplied source timestamp must not be invented from ingestion time. Timestamp precision and timezone uncertainty must be retained; where a source supplies only a date, use a conservative latest-possible publication boundary or exclude the item from intraday research. Effective dates do not establish publication dates. Retrospective corrected records are unavailable for earlier checkpoints unless the original version is archived.

Entity resolution must distinguish similarly named people, traded players, same-day doubleheaders, team rebrands and cross-league identities. Ambiguous matches are excluded, not fuzzy-filled into a forecast. For information pertaining to a player, preserve the team affiliation known at that time and the event to which the item is applicable. Event-independent contract records may later be joined only through that point-in-time affiliation.

Missing values remain null with a reason such as `NOT_COLLECTED`, `PUBLICATION_TIME_UNVERIFIED`, `STALE`, `ENTITY_UNRESOLVED`, `SOURCE_SCOPE_UNVERIFIED`, `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. A recorded negative assertion is distinct from an absence of reports. Null is not neutral sentiment, a healthy player, a guaranteed contract, zero incentives or zero public interest. No unavailable component is silently renormalized into an apparently complete score.

For the present local AISP tape, operational availability is additionally bounded by persistence creation time: `max(observed_at, available_as_of, created_at) <= checkpoint`. This prevents a later historical backfill from becoming earlier operational knowledge. Source clock semantics remain explicit: sportsbook `observed_at` is the provider market's update time, while `available_as_of` is caller-supplied ingestion knowledge time. Prediction-market `provider_observed_at` can be a fetch-clock fallback in an adapter, so its name alone does not prove original provider publication time; `aisp_received_at` must also pass the checkpoint. Actual first-pitch times are unavailable in this export: scheduled start and its known revision history are the documented conservative pregame boundary, not a claim of actual first pitch.

## RFF interpretation and market alignment

RFF expands to **Ripe For Fuckery** as product language. Its scientific definition is a research index of market imbalance and uncertainty. It is not evidence of fixing, fraud, bookmaker intent or actual sportsbook liability. A high value does not itself recommend a bet, and a low value does not establish safety.

The companion JSON fixes exactly ten components at ten points each. Missing or stale evidence contributes no *observed* points, but retains its full unresolved interval: lower bound is the sum of supported points; upper bound adds ten for each unsupported component. These are identification bounds under missing inputs, not sampling confidence intervals. Evidence coverage measures the proportion of component definitions currently computable, not source reliability, probability of correctness or predictive value. The score is `PROVISIONAL_RFF` unless all ten components are supported; absent essential handle evidence always forces that status. Even `COMPLETE_RESEARCH_RFF` remains unvalidated research.

All scale constants are transparent, fixed V1 research assumptions. They are not learned effects, fitted calibration, empirical percentiles or proven thresholds. Changes require a successor contract and separate evaluation, not retrospective optimization on reported outcomes. Zero observed point contribution means a measured transform produced zero; it must never stand for a missing source.

Use a stable canonical A orientation (MLB home for two-team games; explicitly declared participant A otherwise). Preserve market type, period, line/threshold and settlement rules. Moneyline, spread/run line and total are separate series. For three-way or push-sensitive markets, use an explicitly supported full outcome space; never coerce them into a binary pair. Prediction-market disagreements require event/period/outcome/settlement compatibility, including cancellation and extra-inning/overtime rules where relevant. An unmatched contract is unavailable, not a sportsbook substitute.

Reverse line movement requires independent timestamped evidence identifying the public-ticket side. Price history alone supports signed movement, not the public-opposed interpretation. The relief proxy requires coherent same-source handle fractions and both payout prices for the identical two-sided contract/time basis: `net_if_A=1-h_A*d_A`, `net_if_B=1-h_B*d_B`. This assumes total handle normalized to one, mutually exclusive exhaustive outcomes, all represented bets at those decimal payouts, no pushes/voids, no hedging, no bonuses, no commissions and complete measured handle. Display assumed nets and their difference, never actual sportsbook liability. Public-handle data from one population and quotes from an unrelated bookmaker do not satisfy this contract.
