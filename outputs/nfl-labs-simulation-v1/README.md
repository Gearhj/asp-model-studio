## Rules and genuine limitations

Rules were checked against the official [2026NFL Rulebook](https://static.www.nfl.com/image/upload/fl_attachment/league/tqivdkzt9mu6wdgsh1ku.pdf), Rules4,6,11,16. Regulation uses two30-minute halves; regular-season overtime is capped at10minutes, with both possession opportunities under the2025+rule subject to clock expiry, and the defensive-score exception. Historical2018–2024 opening-touchdown rules are represented. Postseason15-minute repeating overtime is explicitly unsupported and is not published by this version.

This is a reduced drive engine, not a play simulator. `endYardline100` is the sampled last scrimmage spot before the terminal play, **not** a physical ball trace or measured terminal ball position. The duration is sampled jointly with that field-position evidence. Quarter boundaries do not terminate possessions. Clock-censored drives receive no invented score or terminal position. Current kickoff touchbacks use the standard35-yard spot; landing-zone20-yard exceptions, onside kicks, detailed timeouts/penalties, turnover-return detail when unobserved and tactical play choice remain limitations. No player, pass, route or tackle identity is invented. Scenario and drive probabilities are raw; they are not described as calibrated M1 probabilities.
