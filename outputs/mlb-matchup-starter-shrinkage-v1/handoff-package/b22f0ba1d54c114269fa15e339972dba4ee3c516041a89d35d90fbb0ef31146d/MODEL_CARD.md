# MLB_MATCHUP_STARTER_SHRINKAGE_CHALLENGER_V1

Research-only, point-score head-to-head shrinkage around the frozen team-only MLB preview anchor. Not final V18 and not promoted. Development 2022–23; one reused 2024 assessment; 2019–21 history. Historical source vintages and target venue are reconstructed. Target actual starter/lineup information is excluded.

The selected 12-meeting, 180-day half-life, 360-day age-limit, minimum-two, strength-32 rule penalizes other-park observations by 0.5 and noisy donors by variance/mean (floor 1). Winner probabilities are copied from the anchor. No joint distribution, interval or wagering authority exists.

Negative result: later-period deviance and total MAE slightly worsen with uncertainty including zero. Starter and combined-with-starter capabilities remain unsupported because the target identity is unavailable at the preview cutoff. These fall back exactly; they are not a fitted starter model. No tail cap is applied. See REPORT.md and ASSESSMENT.json for complete results and limits.
